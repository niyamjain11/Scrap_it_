# E-Waste Taxonomy & Material Recovery Standards 📊

This document details the classification taxonomy, hazard ratings, and precious/base metal yields modeled in **SCRAPIT (Kabadiwala Connect)** according to the **E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022** notified by CPCB / MoEFCC.

---

## 1. Statutory E-Waste Categories

| Code | Category | Examples | Typical Benchmark Rate (₹/kg) | Hazard Severity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ITEW1** | Laptops & Notebooks | Portable PCs, MacBooks, Chromebooks | ₹300 – ₹380 | Medium (Batteries) |
| **ITEW2** | Central Processing Units (CPUs) | Tower PCs, Workstations, Server Blades | ₹280 – ₹350 | Low |
| **ITEW3** | Circuit Boards / Motherboards | High-grade multi-layer PCBs, RAM, GPUs | ₹450 – ₹650 | High (Lead, Brominated Flame Retardants) |
| **ITEW4** | Monitors & Displays | LCD / LED / CRT Panels | ₹120 – ₹180 | High (Phosphor, Mercury backlights) |
| **ITEW5** | Telephones & Cellular Phones | Smartphones, Feature phones | ₹350 – ₹450 | Medium (Cobalt, Lithium) |
| **CEEW1** | Large Household Appliances | Refrigerators, Washing Machines, Air Conditioners | ₹40 – ₹65 | High (CFC/HFC refrigerants) |
| **CEEW2** | Small Household Appliances | Microwaves, Electric kettles, Vacuum cleaners | ₹35 – ₹55 | Low |

## 2. Benchmark Recovery Yields (Per Metric Tonne of High-Grade PCBs)

Formal mechanical and hydrometallurgical recycling recovers critical secondary raw materials at significantly lower carbon emissions than virgin ore mining:

* **Gold (Au)**: $\sim 200\text{--}350\text{ g}$
* **Silver (Ag)**: $\sim 1.0\text{--}1.8\text{ kg}$
* **Palladium (Pd)**: $\sim 80\text{--}120\text{ g}$
* **Copper (Cu)**: $\sim 130\text{--}180\text{ kg}$
* **Aluminum (Al)**: $\sim 60\text{--}90\text{ kg}$
* **Iron / Steel (Fe)**: $\sim 200\text{--}300\text{ kg}$

---

## 3. Environmental Benefits vs. Landfill Dumping

1. **Water Table Protection**: Prevents lead, cadmium, and lithium leaching into groundwater reservoirs.
2. **Atmospheric Health**: Eliminates toxic dioxin, furan, and mercury vapor emissions from backyard open-air acid burning.
3. **Circular Resource Security**: Recirculates rare earth minerals and copper back into Indian manufacturing industries.
